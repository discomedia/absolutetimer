# App Review Information — Absolute Timer 1.1 (Build 7)

Use the first section as the **Notes** field in App Store Connect after the
physical-device recording has been uploaded.

## Notes for App Review

**Screen recording**

A 52-second screen recording captured on a physical iPhone 13 Pro Max running
iOS 26.1 is attached to this version. It starts from the device Home Screen,
launches Absolute Timer, selects the EMOM profile, starts the timer, shows the
active work state, and pauses/resumes the timer.

**Devices and OS versions tested**

- iPhone 13 Pro Max, iOS 26.1 (physical device).
- iPhone 12 Pro Max Simulator, iOS 17.5.

**Purpose and audience**

Absolute Timer is an offline training timer for boxing, MMA, HIIT, EMOM, and
interval workouts. It helps athletes and coaches run repeatable work/rest
rounds with a large readable countdown, automatic round and break transitions,
and optional sound, voice, and haptic cues. It is intended for people who want
a simple, focused workout timer. It is not medical, safety, or coaching advice.

**Setup and main features**

No setup, account, login credentials, purchases, subscriptions, or sample files
are required. Launch the app and select a built-in profile (Standard Boxing,
Standard MMA, EMOM, or E2MOM), or create a custom profile from the profile
selector. Tap Start to run the timer; the app automatically moves between
rounds and breaks. The Settings button controls sound effects, voice
announcements, and haptic feedback.

**External services, tools, and platforms**

None. The app does not use a backend, network API, authentication provider,
payment processor, analytics service, advertising SDK, AI service, or other
third-party SDK. Profiles and settings are stored only on the device using
UserDefaults. Audio cues and text-to-speech use Apple system frameworks.

**Regional availability**

The app has the same features and content in every region. It does not depend
on region-specific services or content.

**Regulated industries and third-party material**

The app is a general fitness/training timer, not a regulated medical service.
It does not include protected third-party content. It uses only system-provided
audio cues and text-to-speech.

**Privacy and permissions**

The app does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone,
photos, HealthKit, tracking, or any other sensitive device data. It does not
collect or track user data.

## Required physical-device recording run sheet

Record in portrait on the iPhone 13 Pro Max running iOS 26.1. Start the
recording while on the Home Screen; then follow this sequence without edits:

1. Tap **Absolute Timer** to launch it.
2. Tap **Standard Boxing**, open the profile selector, choose **EMOM**, and
   return to the timer.
3. Tap **Start**. Show the active green timer for several seconds.
4. Tap **Pause**, then **Start** again to demonstrate resume.
5. Tap **Reset** and choose **Reset Workout** in the confirmation dialog.
6. Tap the profile name, open **Manage Profiles**, tap **+**, enter a name such
   as `Review Demo`, change one timing control, save it, and select it.
7. Return to the timer and tap the **Settings** gear. Show the Sound Effects,
   Voice Announcements, and Haptic Feedback controls, then tap **Done**.
8. Stop recording. Do not include unrelated apps, notifications, credentials,
   or personal data.

The app does not display any permission prompts, account flows, paid content,
user-generated content, reporting/blocking features, or purchase flows,
because it does not have those features.
